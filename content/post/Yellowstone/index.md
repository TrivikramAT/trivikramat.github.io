### Artists Mud pots
Hot acidic water bubbles up from under the ground and dissolves the mud into a sludge. Steam continues to bubble out, causing these blub-blubs which are quite fun to watch. Watch out though!! Some little blubs may splatter onto you (ouch!) or your clothes (souvenir).
