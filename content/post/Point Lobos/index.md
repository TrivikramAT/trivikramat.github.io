## Bird Island
![](DSC06506.jpg)
![](DSC06539.jpg)
![](DSC09716.jpg)
A literal mountain of solidified bird poop aka GUANO. The underlying rock is black, and everything in white is hundreds of years of poop! Guano is super rich in Nitrogen, Phosphorus and Potassium, and was used as plant fertilizer for ages till we invented chemical means of producing fertilizers. Veritasium has an excellent video on the topic - start playback from [here](https://youtu.be/EvknN89JoWo?si=x2q1k5DB979L9Y8U&t=62)
